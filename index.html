01 — Shell

Clear as what it carries.

Tritan Renew has the clarity of glass at a third of the weight. It is half recycled, BPA-free and dishwasher safe, and it keeps no taste behind.

02 — Capacity
750ml

Exactly enough.

Seven hundred and fifty millilitres: a morning’s worth, sized to the hand and the cup holder.

Steel where it matters.

A 316 stainless ring, brushed in one direction, carries the thread. Water only ever touches steel and Tritan.
